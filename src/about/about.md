I build embedded products that people can sell, and the teams that make them.

I care about doing embedded well: a clear architecture, software that is simple to maintain,
and products that keep working in the field for many years. I started at the low level, with
boards, bootloaders and kernels, and I still like to get my hands dirty.

Today I'm Director of Torizon R&D at Toradex. I lead several engineering teams, all of them
very technical, working on an embedded Linux platform for industrial devices: the OS,
containers, over-the-air updates and the tools around them. My job is to set the technical
direction, grow the people, and give them a place where they can do great work.

Good engineering is only half of the job. A product must solve a real problem for the customer
and make sense as a business. I have always had an entrepreneur's mindset, so I think about
the market as much as about the code. At SECO I had the idea for Edgehog OS, built it, and took
it to the first paying customer.

I like to build things, and I like to see people grow while we build them together.

This blog is where I write about:

- **Embedded systems**: boards, bring-up, Linux, and debugging with a scope in one hand.
- **Architecture and products**: design decisions, trade-offs, and building products that last and sell.
- **Teams**: hiring, growing engineers, and leading very technical teams.
